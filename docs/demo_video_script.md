## 📜 Scene-by-Scene Script

---

### Scene 1: Introduction & The Core Problem
**⏱ Time:** 0:00 – 0:40 (40 seconds)  
**🖥️ On-Screen:** Start on the live dashboard at **`https://recoverpayai.web.app`**. Show the header, KPI counters, and the live status badge.

> **Voiceover (Speaker):**  
> "Every single day, businesses lose over 20% of their revenue due to failed digital payments—from temporary bank server glitches and expired cards to network drop-offs.
>
> When a payment fails, traditional systems either repeatedly spam the customer's card—triggering bank fraud blocks—or send generic, ignored emails. The result? Frustrated customers and lost revenue.
>
> Welcome to **RecoverPay AI**—an enterprise autonomous revenue recovery agent. RecoverPay combines **machine learning decision-making**, **deterministic merchant policy boundaries**, and **Razorpay’s payment gateway** to automatically diagnose, prevent, and recover failed payments in real-time."

---

### Scene 2: Live Failure Ingestion & Autonomous AI Diagnosis
**⏱ Time:** 0:40 – 1:30 (50 seconds)  
**🖥️ On-Screen:**  
1. Click the top-right **"Simulate Event"** button.
2. Click **"Bank Timeout (85% Recovery)"**.
3. Point to the live diagnosis trace appearing in the modal.
4. Close the modal, find the case in the **Recovery Queue**, and click **"Inspect AI"**.
5. Scroll through the side drawer showing the **7-Step Reasoning Trace** and **Expected Value (EV)** calculation.

> **Voiceover (Speaker):**  
> "Let’s see it in action. When a customer’s transaction fails, Razorpay fires a real-time webhook directly to RecoverPay.
>
> Let's simulate a temporary **Bank Server Timeout** on a ₹4,999 order. 
>
> Instantly, our autonomous agent wakes up and executes a 7-step diagnosis:
> 1. It fetches the customer's past transaction history and lifetime value.
> 2. It predicts recovery win rates across all 5 recovery strategies using our ML model.
> 3. For a temporary bank glitch, it calculates that a **silent background retry** has an **85% probability of success** without bothering the customer.
> 4. It verifies this action against merchant safety rules, executes the recovery on Razorpay’s API, and records an immutable cryptographic audit log."

---

### Scene 3: Merchant Policy Engine & Industry Presets
**⏱ Time:** 1:30 – 2:20 (50 seconds)  
**🖥️ On-Screen:**  
1. Close the drawer and click the **"Policies"** button in the top navigation bar.
2. Show the modal with **Industry Policy Presets**: click **SaaS**, **E-Commerce**, and **B2B**.
3. Highlight the safety sliders (*Max Auto-Recovery Amount*, *Max Retries*, *Min Probability*).

> **Voiceover (Speaker):**  
> "One size never fits all in payments. A SaaS subscription company wants silent background retries to prevent churn, while an e-commerce store needs instant WhatsApp links to capture impulse shoppers before they leave.
>
> RecoverPay solves this with the **Merchant Policy Engine** and **1-Click Industry Presets**:
> - **SaaS / Subscriptions:** Tolerates higher silent API retries (up to 3 attempts) before emailing.
> - **High-Volume E-Commerce:** Dispatches instant WhatsApp 1-click links to prevent cart drop-off.
> - **B2B High-Ticket:** Enforces strict limits and immediately routes large payments (like ₹75,000+) to human managers for white-glove follow-up.
>
> Merchants can tune thresholds or switch presets with a single click."

---

### Scene 4: Omnichannel Customer Recovery & 1-Click Checkout
**⏱ Time:** 2:20 – 3:10 (50 seconds)  
**🖥️ On-Screen:**  
1. Close the policy modal. In the **Recovery Queue**, click **"Preview"** on an active case.
2. Toggle between **WhatsApp Interactive**, **SMS**, and **Branded Email** tabs.
3. Click **"Pay Securely via Razorpay Gateway"** inside the WhatsApp mockup.
4. In the checkout drawer, choose **UPI (Google Pay / PhonePe)** and click **"Pay Now"**.
5. Watch the success screen with the 3-second auto-close countdown. Show the dashboard updating in real-time.

> **Voiceover (Speaker):**  
> "Now let's look at what the customer experiences when a card expires or funds are temporarily unavailable.
>
> RecoverPay prepares a personalized, brand-verified notification across WhatsApp, SMS, and Email. 
>
> In WhatsApp, the customer gets a friendly message confirming their items are reserved for 24 hours with a 1-click Razorpay payment link.
>
> When they click **'Pay Securely'**, they are presented with a seamless checkout supporting UPI, Credit Cards, and Netbanking. 
>
> When the payment is completed, RecoverPay captures the transaction via webhooks, **clears the item from the recovery queue in real-time**, updates recovered revenue, and seals the transaction in the audit log."

---

### Scene 5: Human-in-the-Loop Escalation & Immutable Audit Trail
**⏱ Time:** 3:10 – 3:55 (45 seconds)  
**🖥️ On-Screen:**  
1. Switch to the **"Human Escalations"** tab. Click **"Resolve Case"** on an item.
2. Show the 4 resolution actions (*Send VIP Link*, *Manual Retry*, *Offline Wire*, *Write Off*) and the mandatory operator notes field.
3. Switch to the **"Immutable Audit Trail"** tab. Scroll down to show SHA-256 integrity checkmarks and click **"Export Audit Log (CSV)"**.

> **Voiceover (Speaker):**  
> "For high-value enterprise transactions that breach policy limits, RecoverPay routes the case to the **Human Escalations Workspace**.
>
> An Operations Lead can review the AI diagnosis and resolve the case by dispatching a custom VIP link, triggering a manual retry, or logging an offline wire transfer. Mandatory resolution notes ensure total accountability.
>
> Every single event—from AI tool calls to human overrides—is sealed in an **Immutable SHA-256 Cryptographically Chained Audit Trail**. This guarantees tamper-proof transparency for financial compliance and SOC 2 audits."

---

### Scene 6: Financial ROI, Role-Based Security & Conclusion
**⏱ Time:** 3:55 – 4:30 (35 seconds)  
**🖥️ On-Screen:**  
1. Switch to the **"Financial Benchmark & ROI"** tab. Adjust the interactive monthly volume slider.
2. Click the **User Role dropdown** in the top navbar and switch from **Merchant Admin** $\rightarrow$ **Operations Lead** $\rightarrow$ **Compliance Auditor** to show role-based locked buttons.
3. Return to the main overview.

> **Voiceover (Speaker):**  
> "Finally, our **Financial Benchmark & ROI Calculator** proves that RecoverPay delivers an average **+180% to +200% recovery uplift** over traditional rule-based retries, projecting millions in saved annual revenue.
>
> With enterprise **Role-Based Access Control** supporting Merchant Admins, Operations Leads, and Compliance Auditors, sensitive settings remain strictly protected.
>
> RecoverPay AI transforms payment failure from a revenue leak into an automated, customer-friendly growth engine.
>
> Thank you!"
